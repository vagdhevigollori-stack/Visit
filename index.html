OUR HERITAGE

The Heart of Tribal India

India's tribal communities have a rich cultural heritage that has been preserved through generations. Their unique traditions, languages, art, clothing, music and connection with nature make India's cultural diversity truly special.

From the forests of Central India to the hills of the Northeast and the Nilgiris, every community has its own beautiful identity and traditions.
